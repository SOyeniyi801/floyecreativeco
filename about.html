THE STORY BEHIND FLOYÉ

Independent by design.
Built to create.

Founded in 2024, Floyé Creative Co. is an Atlanta-based creative studio specializing in web design, development, UX, creative strategy, and video editing.

Led by Sammi, a multidisciplinary creative experience consultant with a background in UX and product design, web development, content systems, and video production, the studio brings both creative vision and technical expertise to every project.

Floyé has worked with clients across technology, nonprofits, e-commerce, entertainment, real estate, and digital media, helping businesses and organizations build stronger digital experiences, communicate their stories, and bring their ideas to life.
